# What's washed ashore

## 1.0.0 — First beach day

- First-to-five soccer tennis with local AI and Easy/Normal/Hard difficulty.
- Sunny, Hopper and Coco share a huge-shoe wardrobe and ten sports animations.
- Direction-sensitive shots, jumping kicks, headers, perfects and power kicks.
- Full menu, selection, pause, results and rematch flow.
- Beach-themed HUD with a persistent controls guide.
- Looping supplied music with an independent on/off toggle.
- Downloadable standalone HTML with all runtime assets embedded.

Known limits: desktop keyboard/mouse only; fixed-camera scenery; character fidelity and broader performance/browser acceptance remain unfinished.
